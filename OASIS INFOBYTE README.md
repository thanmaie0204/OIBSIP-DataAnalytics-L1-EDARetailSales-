# OASIS INFOBYTE Internship
## Level 1 - Task 1: Exploratory Data Analysis on Retail Sales Data

### Project Overview
This project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) 
on retail sales data to identify sales trends, product performance, 
regional performance, and customer purchasing patterns.

### Dataset
The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset, which contains 
information about orders, customers, products, sales, discounts, 
quantities, and profits.

### Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

### Work Performed
- Loaded and inspected the dataset.
- Checked data types, missing values, and duplicate records.
- Calculated descriptive statistics.
- Analyzed sales by product category and region.
- Identified the top 10 products by sales.
- Visualized sales distribution.
- Analyzed monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Examined customer segments.
- Created a correlation heatmap.
- Calculated statistical measures for sales.

### Key Findings
The analysis explores differences in sales and profit across 
product categories, regions, customer segments, and time periods. 
The visualizations help identify patterns and areas for further 
business investigation.

### Business Recommendations
1. Focus on product categories and regions that perform well.
2. Review products or categories with low profitability and evaluate 
   the impact of discounts.
3. Use monthly and quarterly sales trends to support inventory 
   planning and sales forecasting.

### Dataset Limitation
The Sample Superstore dataset does not contain customer age or gender 
columns. Therefore, customer analysis was performed using the available 
customer segment information instead.

### Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Python-based EDA can help summarize 
retail sales data, identify trends, compare business performance, 
and support data-driven decisions.
